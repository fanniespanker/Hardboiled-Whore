# CNML Core Specification v0.1.6

## Foundations

CNML is a constrained XML vocabulary with semantic rules. All CNML documents MUST be well-formed XML.

## Narrative Ontology

- `<narrative>` is not objective reality.
- It is a *projection generated by a narrator model*.
- The narrator may be unreliable, dishonest, delusional, hallucinating, or subject to misremembering.
- All CNML discourse is interpreted relative to this potentially unreliable narratorial projection.

## Discourse

- Default mode: implicit `<narrative>`
- Narrative is the primary container for all discourse, including internal, non-actualized, and temporal variants.
- `<fantasy>`, `<dream>`, `<flashback>`, and `<remember>` are subordinate narrative modalities.
  - These are not separate top-level discourse states.
  - They exist within `<narrative>` as structured cognitive or temporal frames.

## Discourse Units

- Derived from blank-line separation
- Computed per container

## Expression Modality

- `<say>`: spoken utterance
- `<feel>`: low-level cognition / autonomic and limbic state (instinct, hunger, arousal, discomfort, etc.)
  - Replaces prior `<think mode="instinct">`
- `<gesture>`: nonverbal action or embodied expression

## Prosody

Prosody is compositional across inline elements and may stack.

- `<soft>`: attenuation
- `<strong>`: intensity
- `<emph>`: emphasis

## Integrity

- `<redacted>`, `<censored>`, `<glitched>`

## Music DSL

Music DSL is a structured, human-readable temporal annotation layer for expressive or performative text segments.

Music elements may appear as semantic content inside ANY CNML container, including `<say>`, `<feel>`, `<fantasy>`, `<dream>`, `<flashback>`, `<remember>`, and `<narrative>`.

### Core Element

```xml
<music time="" beat="" key="" mode="" tempo="" feel="">
```

### Attributes

- `time`: bar or measure index (qualitative or numeric)
- `beat`: beat grouping or meter unit
- `key`: tonal center (optional)
- `mode`: modal context
- `tempo`: expressive tempo descriptor (may be non-metronomic)
- `feel`: qualitative affective performance descriptor (distinct from `<feel>` cognitive element)

### Semantics

- Music DSL is *non-binding*: it does not enforce timing or execution
- It annotates interpretive performance of textual or vocal content
- It may function as semantic content embedded within other CNML elements
- Attributes may be omitted or underspecified

### Composition Rules

- Music elements may be nested inside any CNML container element
- Multiple `<music>` tags may exist per discourse unit
- Music does not override discourse or prosody semantics

## Nesting Rules

- Nesting of discourse elements (`<say>`, `<feel>`, `<dream>`, `<fantasy>`, `<flashback>`, `<remember>`, etc.) is permitted.
- Individual authors may impose stricter structural constraints, but the CNML specification does not enforce a global nesting restriction.
- Overlapping or recursive narrative cognition structures are valid CNML.
