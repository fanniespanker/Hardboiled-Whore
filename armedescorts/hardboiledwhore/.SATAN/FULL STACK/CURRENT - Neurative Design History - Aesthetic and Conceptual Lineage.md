# 1. Purpose

This document records non-formal but structurally influential ideas, metaphors, and linguistic artifacts that shaped the development of the **Neurative stack**.

In this document, **Neurative** refers to the whole stack and long-term development project, not only to the learner layer previously called Neurative.

These elements are NOT part of the formal specification. They are preserved as design history, cognitive framing artifacts, and interpretive lineage markers.

---

# 2. Naming Note

The overall stack is provisionally named **Neurative** because the long-term goal is an experience-learning, graph-native, ambiguity-preserving cognitive architecture.

The layer previously called **Neurative** is renamed:

```text
AUSPEX — Ambiguity Understanding through Structured Phenomenological EXegesis
```

AUSPEX is the exegetical learner/deformation layer of the Neurative stack. PAM presents ambiguity-preserving candidate readings; CAThedral arranges them into executable herring-bone graph ritual; AUSPEX follows those readings through their deformation trajectories and learns what each interpretation becomes.

Candidate future generative/authorship layers and artifacts include:

```text
SCRIBE — Structured Creative Rendering from Integrated Bibliographic Experience
SCRIPTURE — SCRIBE output artifact / generated authored text
APOCRYPHA — noncanonical draft, variant, discarded branch, or side-text
CANON — accepted / stabilized authored work or internally ratified output
GOSPEL — high-authority self-expressive or doctrine-bearing authored output
HERESY — contradiction, rejected interpretation, adversarial variant, or canon-breaking divergence
AUTEUR — Autonomous Textual Understanding, Expression, and Realization
```

AUTEUR uses **Realization** rather than Rendering because this layer is intended to bring latent artistic intention into concrete authored form, not merely render output.

Current stack components include CNML, C3TCalc, Purple Herring, SARDINE, Herring Bones, GEL, PAM, AUSPEX, DNL/MGL/VPFA/AWS/CME/CPM/MGR, SCRIBE, SCRIPTURE, APOCRYPHA, CANON, GOSPEL, HERESY, and AUTEUR.

# 3. Origin Context

The GEL stack did not emerge purely from formal specification design. It evolved through narrative markup systems, psychological representation models, ambiguity-preserving semantics, and graph-based structural reasoning.

> aesthetics and semantics co-evolved.

# 4. “Pornographtheory” (Origin Coinage)

Term: pornographtheory

Meaning: a compressed description of early exploratory systems involving layered erotic/narrative markup, psychological representation complexity, and overlapping interpretive structures.

Evolutionary significance:

- served as an early conceptual pressure point;
- highlighted inadequacy of tree-based markup for layered cognition;
- contributed to graph-native semantic modeling across CNML, C3TCalc, GEL, PAM, and AUSPEX;
- reinforced ambiguity-preserving representation;
- influenced CNML structural layering;
- contributed to rejection of single-interpretation semantics.

This term is historical linguistic artifact, not operational terminology.

# 5. “Eldritch Catgirls in a Vast Creepy Library”

Pronunciation note:

```text
CAThedral is deliberately pronounced [kætˈhiˌdɹʌl]
```

This pronunciation is a historical/aesthetic joke, not a formal technical requirement. It preserves the catgirl-priest / ritual-execution architecture pun while allowing **GEL** to remain the precise formal name for the graph execution semantics layer.

This phrase describes a conceptual aesthetic that emerged informally during GEL and broader Neurative-stack development.

It refers to:

- GEL canonical representations resembling arcane inscriptions;
- CAThedral as the ritual/execution substack metaphor in which herring-bone graph-script is enacted;
- C3TCalc / Purple Herring fish behaving like ritualized semantic artifacts;
- ontology graphs behaving like maintained library structures;
- Herring Bones functioning as a standard template library of semantic skeletons;
- SARDINE functioning as parser/canonicalizer/tooling fishery;
- increasing structural opacity of explicit graph representations.

Interpretation:

- catgirls = ontology maintainers, fishmongers, validators, semantic librarians;
- library = infinite graph substrate / archival semantic space;
- eldritch = non-intuitive structural depth.

Important: This is not a design requirement or formal metaphor.

# 6. Aesthetic Pressure as a Design Driver

> increasing semantic precision often increases perceived strangeness.

Consequences:

- canonical representations may appear ritualistic or abstract;
- readability is not prioritized over structural fidelity.

---

# 7. Emergent Principle: Structural Uncanny-ness

As systems become more graph-explicit and less ontology-collapsed, human interpretability decreases, structural clarity increases, and aesthetic familiarity decreases.

This tension is not resolved in favor of either side.
