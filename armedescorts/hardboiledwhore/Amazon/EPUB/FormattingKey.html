Formatting Key:

A Note to the Reader
on the Formatting Used
to Hint at Unspoken Thoughts
and Softly Spoken Utterances

The voiced or unvoiced qualities of thoughts, memories, fantasies, whispers, and nonverbal gestures ought to be either implied or denoted explicitly by the bare text, but they have been further hinted at by choice of punctuation, font face, and color:

  • To you I whispered, (Softly spoken utterances could be surrounded by a single pair of parentheses, and have blue text.) I hope you could hear me.
  • I made a face that said, [The meaning of nonverbal gestures could be surrounded by a single pair of square brackets, and have red italicized text.] Not that you could read my mind.
  • I thought to myself, ((My inner stream of consciousness in the moment could be surrounded by doubled parentheses, and the text is magenta and italicized.)) There were more elegant ways to do it, but I had settled on purple cognition.
  • Deep within my loins gurgled a wretched impulse, (((Fuck me fuck me fuck me cum inside me.))) They come to me between three pairs of parentheses, cyan and italicized.
  • I dreamt of formatting vivid remembrances and hallucinations; between a single pair of curly brackets, green as summer foliage and italicized, {You pick me up and swing me around, then cradle me in your arms.} I smile at the memory.
  • I sang to you, “Yellow plain text signals song / be the lyrics right or wrong.” I hoped that I had amused you.
