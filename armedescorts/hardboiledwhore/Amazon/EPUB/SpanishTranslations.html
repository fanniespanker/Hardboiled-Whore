tamal [t̪aˈmal]

n. A portable mesoamerican food, consisting of a boiled cake of masa bound with lard and any of a variety of fillings, wrapped in a corn husk or banana leaf; tamales stuffed with carne de res with an aceituna (traditionally unpitted) predominate the Mexican northwest; other common fillings include carnitas, papas, rajas con queso (cheese and sliced peppers, usually poblanos) and (for dessert) piña. Large numbers are prepared communally for celebrations, particularly Navidad.
